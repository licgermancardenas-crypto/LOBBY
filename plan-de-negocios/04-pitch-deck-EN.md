## Slide 2 — The problem
**Brands spend billions on gaming creators and can't tell if the audience is
real.**
- Influencer marketing ~US$33B (2025); gaming is the fastest-growing vertical.
- 37.2% of influencer followers are fake or suspicious → ~US$4.6B wasted per year.
  Fraud peaks at 48.3% in the 100K–500K tier.
- Every brand's #1 question — *"real or inflated?"* — has no answer at scale.
- Today they pay agencies to find out: manual, expensive, opaque.
- And LATAM creators have no portable professional identity.

---

## Slide 3 — The solution
**Free verified profile for creators · paid verified directory for brands.**
- OAuth-verified audience (Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) = a live media kit.
- Brands search and filter verified LATAM creators by game, country, platform and
  size.
- *(Screenshot: verified profile + brand discovery.)*

---

## Slide 4 — Why now
**Money is moving into gaming, LATAM is huge, and there's no professional layer.**
- Creator economy US$250B (2023) → US$480B (2027) · esports only ~US$3B → the
  money is in content.
- Non-endemic brands overtook endemic ones in gaming sponsorship in 2025.
- LATAM: 372.3M players, US$8.3B games spend, >US$1.1B influencer spend — and no
  identity layer.
- OAuth verification has only recently become cheap.

## Slide 7 — Business model
**A sequenced portfolio — creators never pay; brands do.**
1. Verified B2B directory (first revenue).
2. Recruiting/jobs (the Hitmarker model, proven profitable).
3. Creator freemium.
4. Take-rate on deals.

## Slide 8 — Go-to-market
**Supply density first, then monetize demand.**
- Supply: BD by game and dense market + league partnerships → organic acquisition.
- Demand: direct sales to endemic brands and beverage/QSR brands already investing
  in LATAM; agencies as priority accounts.
- The brand pitch: *"What takes you months and an agency today takes minutes here,
  with verified data."*

---

## Slide 9 — Why we win (moat + others' failures)
- eFuse and Zengaming died: consumer social doesn't monetize. **LOBBY is B2B.**
- Moat: proprietary verification dataset + LATAM category position + two-sided
  network ignited in the right order + switching costs.
