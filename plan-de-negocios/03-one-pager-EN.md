# LOBBY — One-Pager

**The professional layer of the entertainment economy.**
Pre-seed · US$500K · LATAM · pre-launch (product built)

---

**The problem.** Brands already spend billions on gaming influencer marketing,
yet they can't answer their #1 question at scale: *is this audience real or
inflated?* 37.2% of influencer followers are fake or suspicious, and brands waste
~US$4.6B a year on audiences that don't exist — fraud peaks at 48.3% in the
100K–500K tier, exactly the mid-tier LOBBY verifies. Today brands outsource this
to agencies: manual, expensive, opaque. On the other side, LATAM gaming creators
have no portable professional identity — their value is scattered across Twitch,
YouTube, TikTok and a Linktree that proves nothing.

**The solution.** LOBBY gives creators a free professional profile with
**OAuth-verified audience data** (Twitch, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) — a live
media kit that updates itself — and sells **brands and agencies** access to that
verified directory, searchable by game, country, platform and audience size.
B2B discovery-and-verification infrastructure, wrapped in a free profile.

**Why now.** Influencer marketing is ~US$33B (2025), and gaming/entertainment is
its fastest-growing vertical (~32.7% CAGR). Non-endemic brands overtook endemic
ones in gaming sponsorship for the first time in 2025. LATAM has **372.3M
players** and **US$8.3B** in games spend (Newzoo 2025), >US$1.1B in regional
influencer spend growing double digits — and **no consolidated professional
identity layer**. OAuth-based verification has only recently become cheap and
accessible.

**Why us.** Social "LinkedIn for gamers" plays failed (eFuse, Zengaming): gamers
don't pay and cold-start is brutal. LOBBY avoids both: **B2B by design** (the
payer already has budget) and **supply-first sequencing** (build creator density
for free, then charge the brand). The product — verified profiles, discovery,
analytics — **is already built**.

**Business model (sequenced).** (1) Verified B2B directory → first revenue.
(2) Recruiting/jobs (the Hitmarker model, proven profitable). (3) Creator
freemium. (4) Take-rate on deals. Creators never pay for the core: free access
builds the data moat we sell to brands.

**Moat.** Proprietary cross-platform verification dataset + category position in
LATAM + a two-sided network ignited in the right order + switching costs for
brands.

**Traction / status.** Pre-launch. Working MVP: verified-audience profiles
(4 platforms), brand discovery, analytics, automatic data refresh.

**Numbers (base case, illustrative).** Y3 ~US$1.4M ARR · Y5 ~US$11M ARR.
TAM ~US$33B · SAM ~US$189M/yr · SOM ~US$10–15M ARR (5 yrs). B2B unit economics:
LTV/CAC ~18x blended (agencies ~24x), CAC payback ~3.5 months, 85% gross margin.

**The ask.** US$500K pre-seed (SAFE), 18 months of runway. Use of funds: creator
density in 3 markets + first paid B2B pilots. **Seed milestone:** US$250–500K of
contracted B2B ARR + verified creator density in 3 markets.
